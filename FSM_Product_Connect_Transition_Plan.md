## 7. Modernization Methodology: "Modernization with Trust"

### 7.1 Blueprint Framework

**Desired Outcome:** One successful pilot defined with success criteria, owners, and measurable outcomes

**Approach:**
1. **Pick one slice that matters** - One end-to-end business flow and its full dependency chain (apps, integration, data, identity, operations). Example: customer onboarding, claims intake, fraud detection
2. **Align on what "clean" means** - Supported versions, compatibility, controls agreed, rollback defined, audit evidence ready
3. **Execute one upgrade end-to-end** - One IBM owner coordinates IBM product, support, and delivery with client's platform, application, and risk owners through one shared plan, one weekly cadence, one escalation path
4. **Prove it with evidence** - Operational stability results, clean execution of change window, audit-ready artifacts, lessons captured
5. **Create a repeatable pattern** - Capture steps, prerequisites, ownership in simple runbook so next upgrade is faster
6. **Scale in waves** - Repeat proven pattern on next 2-3 targets, then broaden scope once operating model is steady

### 7.2 Why This Matters Now

**What Breaks When You Wait:**
- Business logic stays buried, change cycles get slower
- Decision logic drifts across applications
- Audit prep becomes heavier
- Workflow becomes a hidden bottleneck

**Why an Upgrade Pays Back:**
- Reduce operational and audit risk now
- Supported versions & compatibility
- Position for modernization (containers, hybrid)
- Enables governable orchestration + guardrails
- End-to-end orchestration matters more due to 'agentic disruption'

**Positioning:** A clean upgrade lowers risk now and creates the foundation for faster governable modernization in the future. Risk reduction move, not a feature chase.
